WHO WE ARE

Grown from the ground up

FieldRoots Agri Co. was founded in 2020 by Tafadzwa Makumucha on a 40-acre farm in Harare, Mashonaland Province. What began as a family grain operation has grown into one of Zimbabwe's most trusted agricultural suppliers.

We operate on a simple philosophy: farming should be dignified, profitable, and sustainable. That means paying fair prices to our partner farmers, supplying inputs that actually work, and sharing knowledge freely through our agronomy outreach programme.

Today we supply over 1,400 farmers and buyers across Harare, Gweru and Mutare. Our farm covers 600 hectares under maize, wheat, barley, and vegetables — and we partner with a further 200 smallholder farmers in the surrounding area.
